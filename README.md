## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I start a new game?**
Tap the "+" or "New Game" button on the main screen to create a new game session and add players.

**Can I track scores for multiple games at once?**
Yes, you can have multiple active game sessions and switch between them.

**How do I reset or delete a game?**
Swipe on a game session or use the edit option to delete it.

**Is my data backed up?**
Yes. Your game data automatically syncs to your personal iCloud account, so it stays up to date across your devices. We also recommend keeping regular device backups through iCloud or iTunes.

## Privacy Policy

GameNight: Scorekeeper respects your privacy.

**Data Storage:** Your game data is stored on your device and automatically synced to your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your own iCloud storage and is not accessible to the developer.

**Photo Import:** When you use the photo import feature, images are processed on your device. Photos are not uploaded to external servers.

**Data Collection:** The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data with the developer or third parties.

**Third-Party Services:** The app uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) for data sync. Apple's privacy policy governs the handling of your iCloud data.

For questions about your privacy, please contact us at the email above.
