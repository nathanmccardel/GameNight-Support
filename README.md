## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I start a new game?**
Tap the "+" or "New Game" button on the main screen to create a new game session and add players.

**Can I track scores for multiple games at once?**
Yes, you can have multiple active game sessions and switch between them.

**How do I reset or delete a game?**
Swipe on a game session or use the edit option to delete it.

**Is my data backed up?**
Your data is stored locally on your device. We recommend keeping regular device backups through iCloud or iTunes.

## Privacy Policy

GameNight: Scorekeeper respects your privacy. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All game data is stored locally on your device. For more details, please contact us at the email above.
